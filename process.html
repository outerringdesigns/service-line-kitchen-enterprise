Our Process

From breakdown to a clear decision

Four steps from your repair request to a decision you can make with the facts. For a major failure, you get a written repair-or-replace recommendation before you approve the work.

Request Equipment Repair

Dispatch will call to confirm availability and next steps.

The four steps

  1. 1

    Submit a repair request

    Fill in the short request form: business name, service ZIP code, equipment type, a short description of the problem, and a callback number. It takes about a minute.

    Helpful to include

    The make and model, gas or electric, and any error codes. See what to include.

  2. 2

    Dispatch calls you

    Dispatch calls to confirm availability and next steps. They may ask follow-up questions about the equipment and the problem.

    Have ready

    Your phone nearby, and access to the equipment’s data plate if you can reach it safely.

  3. 3

    A technician diagnoses the equipment

    A technician inspects and tests the equipment to find the cause of the problem.

    You’ll learn

    What failed and whether it’s a minor fix or a major failure.

  4. 4

    You get a written recommendation for major failures

    For a major failure, you receive a written repair-or-replace recommendation before you approve the work. You decide which option to go with.

    Nothing proceeds until

    You’ve reviewed the recommendation and approved the work.
