Equipment we service

Cooking and dishwashing equipment

We repair and maintain commercial kitchen equipment in three main categories. Tell us the make, model, and what’s happening in the request form, and dispatch will confirm whether we can take the job.

  • 01

    Ovens and ranges

    Convection ovens, deck ovens, and commercial ranges that won’t heat, light, or hold temperature.

  • 02

    Fryers

    Commercial fryers with ignition, heating, thermostat, or control problems.

  • 03

    Commercial dishwashers

    Dish machines that won’t fill, drain, heat, or complete a cycle.

Coverage depends on the specific make, model, and parts availability. Not sure? Choose “Other” in the form and describe the equipment.

The repair-or-replace difference

See both options in writing before you approve the work

When a diagnosis finds a major failure, Service Line puts a written recommendation in front of you before any work is approved. You see what a repair involves next to what a replacement involves, so you can weigh cost and downtime and choose.

Our process

How a repair request works

  1. 1

    Submit a repair request

    Tell us your business, ZIP code, equipment, the problem, and a callback number.

  2. 2

    Dispatch calls you

    Dispatch calls to confirm availability and next steps.

  3. 3

    A technician diagnoses it

    A technician inspects the equipment and identifies the problem.

  4. 4

    You decide with the facts

    For a major failure, you get a written repair-or-replace recommendation before approving the work.
